Obsidian is an origination and development firm operating across the four markets behind AI infrastructure — the site, the power that serves it, the silicon that fills it, and the capital that funds all three. One counterparty across all four layers. We are not a marketplace, and we do not publish a price list.

Four markets. One deal. Most of them die in the gaps.

The land people don't speak turbines. The GPU people don't read interconnection queues. The capital asks a question nobody on the deal can answer, and ninety days evaporate.

The problem

A gigawatt of capacity is not one transaction.

It is a site with a defensible interconnect position, generation deliverable on the schedule the tenant underwrote, hardware that is contracted rather than promised, and capital that understands all three well enough to price the risk. Obsidian works across all four.

The four layers

What we transact.

Obsidian holds live positions across each layer. A mandate can start at any one of them — most start at power.

I

Infrastructure

Powered land, powered shells and operating facilities, underwritten on energization date, queue position and deliverability rather than a brochure.

  • Powered land and shells, 5 MW to multi‑hundred MW
  • Operating data centers and colocation portfolios
  • Brownfield — industrial, manufacturing, legacy telecom
  • Mining sites repositioning to HPC and AI workloads
  • Greenfield campuses with interconnection position
  • Design-build, commissioning and on-site O&M partners

Queue position and study status. Interconnection agreement, executed or not. Title, easements and water rights. Electrical topology to the rack. We would rather kill a site in week two than introduce it in week ten.

C

Compute

Allocation is only worth what the ship date and the paper behind it are worth. We verify both before anything reaches a buyer.

  • NVIDIA B300 and GB300 NVL72 inventory and allocation
  • H200 and Hopper-class systems, new and secondary
  • Contracted bare-metal and neocloud capacity, by term
  • Secondary positions and residual value structures
  • Networking, interconnect and integration partners
  • Deployment partners for rack integration and burn-in

Chain of title on the allocation. Named ship windows rather than quarters. Whether the OEM relationship survives assignment. An allocation that cannot be energized on schedule is inventory, not capacity.

E

Energy

Power is the binding constraint on every transaction in this market. It is the first thing we diligence and the last thing we take on faith.

  • Behind-the-meter gas, grid-connected and hybrid generation
  • Turbine, reciprocating engine and genset positions
  • BESS and long-duration storage for firming
  • PPAs, tolling structures and curtailment arrangements
  • Substation, transmission and switchyard delivery
  • On-site operations, maintenance and fuel management

Firm versus interruptible, and what the contract says under load. Fuel supply and transport. Equipment lead times against the construction schedule. Every schedule in this business is a power schedule wearing a different hat.

$

Capital

Capital is not a referral we make at the end. It sits at the table from the first conversation, which is why our deals are structured to be financeable before they are marketed.

  • Project and development equity
  • Construction, bridge and take-out debt
  • Equipment and GPU-backed financing
  • Sponsor equity, joint venture and platform capital
  • Sale-leaseback and lease structures
  • M&A for operating platforms and portfolios

Tenant covenant, contract tenor, residual assumptions, and where in the stack the risk is genuinely sitting. A deal that cannot be financed is not a deal; it is a press release waiting to be retracted.

Power is the schedule.

Firm or interruptible, fuel and transport, equipment lead times against the construction programme. Everything else in the transaction moves when this does.

Geography

Our coverage follows power, not postcodes.

Energy-abundant corridors

West Texas and the Permian, Appalachian gas, the Upper Midwest. Slower fiber, faster electrons.

Stranded and behind-the-meter

Power already built for something else. Legacy industrial load, flare gas, and interconnects worth more than their hashrate.

Constrained metros

Northern Virginia, Dallas, Phoenix, Chicago, London. Power is scarce, which is why a defensible queue position is worth underwriting carefully.

How we work

Four commitments we will hold to in writing.

We verify before we introduce.

Nothing reaches a counterparty until we have read the paper ourselves. If diligence weakens the deal, you hear it from us first.

One side of a deal, and we name it.

We represent the buyer or the seller on any given transaction, never both, and the mandate states which before work begins.

Confidentiality by default.

Assets, allocations and capital sources are not shopped. Nothing moves, and no name is used, without written permission.

We don't broker what we wouldn't own.

If an asset doesn't clear our own underwriting, we decline the mandate rather than pass the risk to someone who trusts us.

Constraint is the only real asset class.

Land is available. Capital is available. Silicon is available. What is scarce is the party who can clear all four constraints at once and put a date on it. That is the whole of our business.
